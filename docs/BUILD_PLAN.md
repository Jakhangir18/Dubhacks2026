# Build plan

Deadlines: **Devpost draft Sat 22:30** (all members added, tracks declared, or no judging); final submission **Sun 10:00**; judging Sun 11:30-14:00 in person.

## Roles (team of up to four)

| Role | Owns | First spike (pass condition) |
|---|---|---|
| Vision | camera (webcam or iPhone), pose, gait events | in floor mode and on the walking pad, a strike for every step and a lift above 10 cm only when the player lifts on purpose |
| Game | engine, renderer, art, projector calibration | two-lane loop playable with the keyboard; projected corners land on the floor mat and the targets are visible in hall light |
| Hardware | projector placement and white mat, camera placement, belt stripes, stop button, support rail | projected garden readable in the hall; belt speed within about 10% of the pad's display |
| AI and Visa | coach rules within therapist limits, voice, parent report, rewards, Visa sandbox, Devpost | coach returns a valid cue and parameters inside the limits; a sandbox payment or payment link is created |

## Demo checklist

- Floor mode is the main demo; walking-pad mode only with a spotter, low speed and the stop button.
- Projector focused on the white mat, corners calibrated, hall light tested; screen mode ready as fallback.
- Keyboard mode ready if the camera fails; mock payout ready if the network fails.
- 90-second script rehearsed (see [GAME_DESIGN.md](GAME_DESIGN.md)).

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Camera misses steps | side view, coloured shoe tape, calibrate on the real floor and pad first |
| Projector (about 150 lm) washed out by hall lighting | white mat, small image, dark corner, bright art on black, screen mode fallback |
| Someone falls | children only in floor mode; walking pad for 14+ with a spotter, low speed, rail and stop button |
| Visa sandbox credentials late | create the account now; labelled mock as fallback |
| Medical claims | wellness wording (coordination, balance, mobility); "a tool for the therapist", never "treats" or "restores" |
| Trademark | own names and art; no Plants vs. Zombies assets |
