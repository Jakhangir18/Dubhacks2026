# Venue rig: cardboard booth and white board

A 60-150 lumen projector disappears in a lit hall (200-500 lux): white reaches only about 1.2:1 contrast. Three measures together make it readable; any one alone does not. All numbers are estimates.

## Why

- **White board** (matte, reflectance about 0.85 vs about 0.08 for the black belt): white on the board looks 8-10 times brighter than on the belt.
- **Smaller picture**: 1.6 m² down to about 0.75 m² doubles the light per square metre.
- **Booth** (three cardboard walls and a roof, lined black): cuts room light on the board from about 300 to 30-60 lux, giving 3:1 at 60 lumens and 5:1 at 150.

## Rig

- **Board**: two white foam boards 20 x 30 in (51 x 76 cm), taped end to end on the back: 76 cm wide x 102 cm long, rear edge against the pad's front lip, painter's tape at the corners. Matte: cover glossy board with white printer paper.
- **Belt**: nothing on it (it moves). White poster-board strips 6 x 60 cm on both side rails, white tape across the deck's front lip (a physical hit line), a 5 cm tape mark on each rail at the toe line.
- **Booth**: two side walls 120 x 90 cm, a roof 80 x 120 cm on the walls, a back wall 80 x 90 cm at the far end with a 15 x 15 cm hole for the lens at 85-90 cm and a 5 x 8 cm hole 20 cm lower for the iPhone. Inside lined with cut-open black trash bags. Player end open; judges look over the 90 cm walls.
- **Projector**: on a chair plus a box behind the back wall, lens about 0.9 m high and 0.9-1.0 m past the board's far edge; on its side (portrait) in a cardboard cradle with rubber bands, a cardboard wedge for 30-40 degrees of tilt, vents clear; keystone 0 (the game's warp does the rest). Slide it until the picture just covers the board.
- **iPhone**: in the lower hole, in a slotted cardboard stand about 0.65 m high, below the lens.

## Build (two people, about 100 minutes)

1. 0-10 min: measure deck width, toe line, lens height; tape the marks.
2. 10-25: A joins the boards, cuts rail strips, tapes the hit line. B opens boxes into walls and roof.
3. 25-45: B cuts the back wall, holes, cradle, wedge, phone slot. A lines walls and roof with trash bags.
4. 45-60: assemble the booth around the board; tape the corners; roof on.
5. 60-80: projector on chair and box, portrait, aimed, picture shrunk to the board; phone in its slot.
6. 80-100: game setup, play 3 minutes, fix sagging with tape. Test under the brightest light available.

## Shopping list (about $40-50)

- 2 white foam boards 20 x 30 in ($8-12), 2 white poster boards 22 x 28 in ($3)
- black contractor trash bags ($8-12), duct tape ($6), painter's tape ($5), box cutter ($4), rubber bands or binder clips ($3)
- 4-5 large cardboard boxes: free from the organizers (pizza and shipping boxes)
- Near UW (check hours): Target on University Way NE; Walgreens or Bartell on the Ave; Safeway or QFC on NE 45th; Artist & Craftsman Supply and the University Book Store open Sunday morning.

## Game setup for the rig

- Open the game with `?floor=1` (add `&demo=1` for the short judge version): the whole field goes on the board, the hit line sits at the board's rear edge by the pad, nothing is dimmed as "belt".
- Press Z. Back corners at the board's rear edge (against the pad lip), front corners at the board's far edge. Reset puts the corners back if one gets lost.
- `[` and `]` make the plants slower or faster.

## Fallback

- Shrink the picture again to about 45 x 70 cm (lanes only).
- Laptop upright on the chair facing the player showing the game; the projector only draws the hit line and halos.
- Ask the organizers for a corner with a light switch or a curtain.
- Open the pitch with the dark-room video from Saturday night, then let the judge walk.
